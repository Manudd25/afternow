🌍 AfterNow

Visualizing how our cities could change in a warming world.

AfterNow is an interactive web application that explores how climate change may affect cities over time through an immersive timeline. Rather than providing exact forecasts, the application visualizes multiple possible climate futures using historical climate data and scenario-based projections inspired by current climate research.

🧠 How it works

AfterNow combines historical climate observations with scenario-based projection models to illustrate how environmental conditions could evolve under different greenhouse gas emission pathways.

Users can switch between different climate scenarios to understand how future choices may influence:

Average temperatures
Extreme heat days
Heatwave intensity
Vegetation
Water resources
Air quality
Overall city appearance

The application is designed as an educational visualization rather than a scientific forecasting tool.

📊 Climate Scenarios

🌱 Optimistic

Represents a future with strong global emissions reductions and successful climate mitigation efforts.

🌍 Current Trajectory

Represents a continuation of today's global climate and emissions trends.

🔥 High Emissions

Explores a future where greenhouse gas emissions remain high, resulting in stronger warming and more frequent extreme weather.

---

⚠️ Disclaimer

Future projections shown in AfterNow are illustrative scenarios inspired by historical climate data and established climate research.

They are intended to help users understand potential climate futures and should not be interpreted as exact scientific predictions for individual cities.
